# MeshyGrab

> **Download your Meshy 3D models as GLB — simply and quickly.**

MeshyGrab is an independent browser extension that makes it easier to download your generated 3D models from your Meshy workspace.

Instead of dealing with complicated workflows, MeshyGrab keeps the experience simple:

**Generate → Select your model → Open MeshyGrab → Download GLB**

MeshyGrab is designed as a lightweight companion for creators who want a straightforward way to keep their generated 3D assets.

> **MeshyGrab is an independent third-party product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meshy.**

---

## ✨ What is MeshyGrab?

Meshy is an AI-powered 3D generation platform for creating 3D models from text, images, and other creative inputs.

MeshyGrab focuses on one simple part of that workflow:

**getting your generated model onto your computer as a GLB file.**

The extension works alongside your existing Meshy workspace and does not require you to manually enter API credentials or configure a separate API integration.

---

## 🚀 How It Works

Using MeshyGrab takes only a few steps:

```text
Open Meshy
     ↓
Select your generated model
     ↓
Open MeshyGrab
     ↓
Preview your model
     ↓
Download GLB
```

That's it.

No complicated setup.

No API-token configuration.

No developer knowledge required.

## 🔐 Privacy

MeshyGrab is designed to collect only the information required to provide the product and manage download entitlements.

The extension does not require users to provide their Meshy API token.

The actual model download is performed by the extension rather than routing the model file through MeshyGrab's servers.
